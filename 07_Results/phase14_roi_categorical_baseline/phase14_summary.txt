Interpretation rules
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROI-level metrics treat each valid annotation region as one prediction unit.
Case-level metrics average all ROI probability vectors from the same patient case.
Macro F1 and balanced accuracy are important because the TI-RADS classes are imbalanced.
Quadratic weighted kappa and mean absolute class error are included because TI-RADS categories are ordered.
