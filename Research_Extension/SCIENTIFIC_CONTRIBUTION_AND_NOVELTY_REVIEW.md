# QuCNet Revisited — Scientific Contribution and Novelty Gate

**Prepared:** 28 October 2026. **Gate:** 1 of the pre-publication research review. **State:** Targeted literature review completed; specific contribution defensible, **venue-level novelty still requires guide/peer judgment**. **This is an audit and decision record, NOT IEEE manuscript text.**

**Working research identity:** *QuCNet Revisited: An Independent Reproduction and Methodological Audit of Thyroid Ultrasound Classification*.

**Scope rule:** The project is an independent *QuCNet/DDTI method-fidelity, data-provenance and evaluation-reliability audit*, not a novel optimized QCNN, not an exact reproduction of the source authors' test sets, and not a clinical diagnostic validation. All research claims must distinguish image, ROI, recorded case ID and confirmed patient, and should use only measurements archived on `research/bn-recalibration-audit`.

## 1. Exact scientific question

> Under the source metadata and implementation details independently recoverable for the 2024 QuCNet thyroid-ultrasound study, what can actually be reproduced or verified, which sources of experimental uncertainty constrain comparability, and how sensitive are six-class TI-RADS model conclusions to source labels, preprocessing, classifier family, simulated fixed feature maps, case-ID grouping and case-fold assignments?

**Null or skeptical interpretation:** A single attractive case-accuracy metric need not be a robust effect, a source-exact reproduction, or diagnostic evidence. A measured quantum-feature advantage over a matched classical feature map must be reported with classifier, fold, class-support and probability-reliability context.

This is a *reproducibility and reliability question*, not a hypothesis that quantum computing produces a better classifier.

## 4. Reviewer challenge / required answer map

1. **Why another QML benchmark?** Because the object of study is an existing influential *thyroid ultrasound* QuCNet claim, including dataset/label provenance, source-to-implementation fidelity, cache implementation and casewise reliability, not just swapping quantum and classical heads on an unrelated benchmark.
2. **Did the reproduced system fail the published 97.63%/93.87% numbers?** No direct comparison is valid: different sample denominators, labels, ROIs, source inventory, backend/configuration and split units. The archived model did poorly in its *own* explicitly stated evaluations; source-paper accuracy is attributed to its authors.
3. **Does the quantum mapping improve classification?** Neither a single best seed nor the five-seed descriptive mean establishes a general advantage. Report matched paired tests, all seeds, classwise failure and the majority reference. Circuit is fixed/simulated.
4. **Is the experiment patient-independent?** Not proven. Case-ID exclusivity was independently verified, while patient uniqueness across IDs is unknown. This is a material limitation.
5. **Why did the ROI CNN collapse?** The failure is documented; BatchNorm, preprocessing and cache audits are exploratory and do not isolate a proven cause. Do not invent mechanistic causation.
6. **Where is clinical relevance?** The present work is *methodological*: diagnosing unreliable evaluation/comparability. There is no pathology-confirmed malignancy result or clinical deployment claim.
7. **Did you adjust for all exploratory comparisons?** No. Tests, case bootstraps and confidence intervals are exploratory and affected by outcome-informed design; do not promote isolated uncorrected p-values to confirmatory discovery.
8. **What is independently rerunnable?** Derived-statistics/metadata audits run from sparse GitHub checkout without raw images. Reproducing initial training requires the exact available DDTI snapshot, locally managed environment and documented historical models; full end-to-end source-exact training is not certified.
9. **What is the original intellectual contribution versus prior medical QML papers?** The QuCNet-specific *joint* source-fidelity, TI-RADS provenance, case-metadata, cache integrity and fold/probability failure audit. Generic QML, risk-aware CV and reliability scoring are all precedented.

## 5. Contribution decision and gate disposition

**Defensible working scientific thesis:** The results of QuCNet-style thyroid ultrasound classification cannot be understood from headline accuracy alone: independent reconstruction highlights source-protocol mismatch and case/label provenance ambiguity, while matched feature-map comparisons, repeated case-fold evaluation and probability/classwise audits expose uncertainty and class-dependent failure. The study is an **independent methodological audit with negative and mixed results**, not a new clinical model.

**For guide review:** Present C1 (source fidelity), C2 (case/label provenance), C3 (cache implementation) and C4–C5 (matched controls and reliability) as the main technical story; C6 supports reproducibility. Seek a judgment on whether this QuCNet-specific combined contribution meets the intended journal's threshold for novelty and scope. Do not assert reviewer or journal acceptance in advance.

**Gate 1 disposition:** **Internal scientific positioning completed.** Literature screen and contribution/claim boundaries documented with dated sources. **Final scholarly originality and suitability are not certified** by an assistant and depend on fresh literature checking and guide/editor/peer review. The project is ready for the next gate *after* this record is reviewed; do not start the IEEE manuscript until explicitly authorized.
